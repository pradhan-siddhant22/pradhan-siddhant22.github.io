**Baxter International Inc.**  
*R&D Hardware and Cyber Engineering Intern · Summer 2026 · Round Lake, IL*

Designed and built a working two-factor access control prototype for the lockbox on the NovumIQ PCA infusion pump as part of the Enterprise Engineering, Interoperability team, replacing mechanical key access with NFC card plus PIN authentication so the system could track who opened it and when. Developed modular embedded firmware on an ESP32 integrating an NFC reader, keypad, and linear actuator latch, with timestamped logging of every access attempt. Secured stored credentials using AES-256 encryption with HMAC integrity checks, validated the full workflow against failure cases like unrecognized cards, incorrect PINs, and failed integrity checks, and worked closely with the mechanical team so the security design actually fit the physical enclosure. Documented the design rationale, risk considerations, and the path toward custom PCB and pump integration.
