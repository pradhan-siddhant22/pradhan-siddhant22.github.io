I’m Siddhant, a Computer Engineering senior at UMass Amherst (graduating Spring 2027) with a minor in Engineering Management. I work across the stack, from PCB design and embedded firmware to full-stack web development and machine learning, and I’m most energized by problems that sit at the intersection of hardware and software.

This past summer I was an R&D Hardware and Cyber Engineering Intern at Baxter International Inc., where I designed and built a two-factor access control prototype for the lockbox on a PCA infusion pump. I’m also a researcher at the UMass Human Performance Lab studying how truck drivers interact with automated vehicle technology through VR simulation, and before Baxter I was an AI Software Engineer Fellow at Minds With Purpose Foundation, where I built mobile and web features for a social accountability platform.

I care about building things that work reliably and serve people well, and I'm at my best when the work has real stakes behind it and getting something wrong would actually matter to someone.

When I’m not at a keyboard, I’m probably catching up on movies and TV shows, watching or playing cricket and soccer, or building something out of LEGO.
